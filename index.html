About Me

“

Every individual deserves a safe, compassionate, and non-judgmental space to explore their experiences, overcome challenges, and foster emotional well-being.

Hello, I am Dr. Angana Mukherjee Sharma, an RCI-registered Clinical Psychologist with over 15 years of experience in mental health care, psychological assessment, psychotherapy, and clinical supervision. My work is guided by the belief that every individual deserves a safe, compassionate, and non-judgmental space to explore their experiences, overcome challenges, and foster emotional well-being.

I hold an M.Phil. in Clinical Psychology from the University of Calcutta and have had the privilege of training and working at some of India's leading academic and healthcare institutions, including the University of Calcutta, the National Institute of Mental Health and Neurosciences (NIMHANS), Bengaluru, and Kokilaben Dhirubhai Ambani Hospital, Mumbai. These experiences have enriched my clinical expertise and provided me with extensive exposure to diverse mental health concerns across different populations and settings.

Over the years, I have worked with adolescents, adults, couples, and families experiencing anxiety, depression, stress, relationship difficulties, trauma, emotional challenges, and life transitions and had multicultural exposures. My therapeutic approach is integrative and evidence-based, drawing from Cognitive Behaviour Therapy (CBT), Dialectical Behaviour Therapy (DBT), Behaviour Therapy, Psychodynamic Psychotherapy, Existential Therapy, and Couple and Family Therapy. I believe in tailoring treatment to each individual's unique needs, strengths, and goals.

Alongside my clinical practice, I am actively engaged in research, training, and professional development. I am committed to providing ethical, culturally sensitive, and client-centered care that promotes resilience, self-understanding, and meaningful change.

Therapeutic Philosophy

What Therapy Means to Me

“

Therapy is not about giving answers — it is about helping individuals discover clarity, understanding, and growth within themselves.

I believe that therapy is a process that helps clients grow and learn through their experiences. The therapist acts as a mirror, helping individuals gain an objective understanding of their situations, emotions, relationships, and the people around them.

Through greater awareness and insight, clients are empowered to make meaningful changes that enhance their emotional well-being and improve their overall quality of life.

Psychotherapy is a collaborative process that requires commitment, trust, and active participation from both the client and the therapist. Together, this partnership creates a foundation for growth, resilience, and lasting change.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions you may have before beginning therapy.

Common questions about therapy, confidentiality, appointments, online consultations, and the therapeutic process.

The first session focuses on understanding your concerns, personal history, current challenges, and goals for therapy. It is also an opportunity to discuss expectations, ask questions, and collaboratively determine the most appropriate therapeutic approach for your needs.

A standard therapy session typically lasts between 45 and 60 minutes. The duration may vary depending on the nature of the consultation, assessment requirements, and individual therapeutic needs.

Yes. Confidentiality is a fundamental aspect of the therapeutic relationship. Information shared during sessions remains private, except in circumstances where disclosure is required by law or when there is a risk of harm to yourself or others.

The number of sessions varies for each individual and depends on the nature of the concerns, therapeutic goals, and progress made during treatment. Some clients benefit from short-term support, while others may engage in longer-term therapy.

Yes. Both online and in-person consultations are available. Online therapy provides a convenient and effective option for individuals who prefer remote sessions or are unable to attend in person.

Fees vary depending on the type of service, consultation format, and assessment requirements. Please get in touch directly for current fee details and appointment availability.
