Professional psychological evaluations designed to understand cognitive abilities, personality patterns, learning difficulties, and diagnostic concerns. Assessments provide valuable insights to guide therapeutic planning and personal development.

Cognitive Assessment

Evaluating intellectual functioning, cognitive strengths, and learning patterns to understand abilities and potential areas of difficulty.

✨

Personality Assessment

Comprehensive evaluation of personality traits, emotional patterns, coping styles, and behavioral tendencies for deeper self-understanding.
