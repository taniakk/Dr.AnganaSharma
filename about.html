Professional Profile

A journey dedicated to mental health and psychological well-being.

I hold an M.Phil. in Clinical Psychology from the University of Calcutta and have had the privilege of training and working at some of India's leading academic and healthcare institutions.

My professional journey includes valuable experience at the University of Calcutta, NIMHANS Bengaluru, and Kokilaben Dhirubhai Ambani Hospital Mumbai.

These experiences have enriched my clinical expertise and provided extensive exposure to diverse mental health concerns across different populations, cultures, and clinical settings.

Over the years, I have worked with adolescents, adults, couples, and families experiencing anxiety, depression, stress, trauma, relationship concerns, emotional challenges, and life transitions.

What Therapy Means To Me

"I believe that therapy is a process that helps clients grow and learn through the process and the therapist is like a mirror who helps to get an objective opinion about the situation, life, people around us which in turn helps to improve their quality of life."

Psychotherapy includes the compliance from both the client and the therapist. Together, meaningful and lasting change becomes possible.

Personalized Care

Therapeutic Approach

Tailored to Individual Needs

Since every individual is different, the therapeutic approach is tailored-made based on the client and their goals. There is no one-size-fits-all approach in therapy. My practice is guided by the principle that each person brings their own unique experiences, strengths, challenges, and aspirations to the therapeutic relationship.

Evidence-Based Integration

My therapeutic approach is integrative and evidence-based, drawing from multiple therapeutic modalities including Cognitive Behaviour Therapy (CBT), Dialectical Behaviour Therapy (DBT), Behaviour Therapy, Psychodynamic Psychotherapy, Existential Therapy, and Couple and Family Therapy. This flexibility allows me to adapt treatment strategies to match each client's presenting concerns and therapeutic needs.

Personalized Treatment Planning

I believe in tailoring treatment to each individual's unique needs, strengths, and goals. The therapeutic process is collaborative, with continuous assessment and adjustment of strategies based on client progress, feedback, and evolving needs. This ensures that therapy remains meaningful, relevant, and effective throughout the therapeutic journey.

Providing compassionate, evidence-based psychological care, psychotherapy, psychological assessments, and clinical supervision to support emotional well-being, resilience, and meaningful personal growth.
